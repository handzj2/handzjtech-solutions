# SEO CONTRACT

## Status
Deferred beyond Increment 1. Sitemap, robots.txt, and JSON-LD structured data are explicitly NOT built in Phase 5 Increment 1.

## Requirements (for a later increment)
- Unique page title and meta description per route.
- Canonical URL per route.
- Open Graph metadata per route.
- XML sitemap.
- robots.txt.
- Appropriate structured data (Organization schema may be used without an address).
- Internal linking between related pages.
- Search Console verification after production deployment.

## Rules
- Primary market is Uganda — natural Uganda/service-area terminology only, no fabricated address, coordinates, or city "office" claims.
- No LocalBusiness structured data requiring an address until one is verified.
- No fabricated ratings, reviews, or aggregate scores.
